ELI5 Learner

Privacy policy

ELI5 Learner is a desktop app with an optional Android companion. It has no server of its own, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. This page explains what data it touches and where that data goes.

In short: your documents stay on your devices and, if you turn on sync, in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only this app can read. The authors never receive any of it.

What the app stores

  • The documents you generate and their version history, in the app's folder on your Mac.
  • Your AI provider API key, in the macOS Keychain.
  • If you turn on sync: a Google sign-in token, in the macOS Keychain on the desktop and in Android's account system on the phone.

What leaves your device

  • To the AI provider you choose (Anthropic or OpenAI): the source material you give the app and the instructions needed to write your document, sent with your own API key. That provider's own privacy policy applies.
  • To public web pages you add as sources: an ordinary request to fetch the page.
  • To your Google Drive, only if you turn on sync: your documents and their versions.

Nothing is sent to the authors of ELI5 Learner, and there is no telemetry.

Google user data

Sync is off by default. When you turn it on, the app asks Google for one permission: drive.appdata, which lets it see and manage only its own hidden app-data folder in your Drive. It cannot see, open or list any of your other Drive files, and it does not request Gmail, Photos, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google data.

  • Use: the app-data folder is used only to copy your ELI5 Learner documents between your own devices.
  • Sharing: this data is not shared with, sold to or read by anyone else, and it is not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Storage: it lives in your own Google account and counts toward your Drive storage.

ELI5 Learner's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Turn off sync in the app's settings, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the synced copies in Google Drive on the web: Settings, then Manage apps, then ELI5 Learner, then Delete hidden app data.
  • Delete local documents from the app's library, or uninstall the app.
