# Q3 paid campaign review: Widget Pro launch

Synthetic document for evaluation. Example Widgets Inc. is a fictional company.

## Headline

The launch campaign spent $240,000 and returned $912,000 in attributed revenue, a ROAS of 3.8. Paid search carried the result; paid social underperformed.

## Channel results

| Channel | Spend | Attributed revenue | ROAS | CTR | CAC |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paid search | $120,000 | $600,000 | 5.0 | 4.1% | $95 |
| Paid social | $80,000 | $152,000 | 1.9 | 0.8% | $310 |
| Display | $40,000 | $160,000 | 4.0 | 0.3% | $140 |

## What we learned

- Search captured people already looking for widgets, so intent and CTR were high.
- Social reached new audiences cheaply per impression, but few clicked and fewer bought. CAC on social was more than three times search.
- Display ROAS looks strong, but most of its revenue came through last-click attribution on retargeting ads shown to people who had already visited the site. A 7-day view-through window would credit display with only $60,000.

## Decisions

1. Move $50,000 from paid social to paid search for Q4.
2. Run a holdout test on display: pause it in two regions for four weeks to measure its real lift.
3. Switch reporting from last-click to a data-driven attribution model by January.
