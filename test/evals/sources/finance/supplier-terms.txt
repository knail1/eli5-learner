Procurement note: second housing supplier (synthetic, for evaluation; Example Widgets Inc. is fictional)

Why a second supplier
The single housing supplier caused two line stoppages last year. Adding a second supplier cuts that risk. Volume will be split 70/30 in the first year.

Payment terms
Invoices are payable net 60, meaning within 60 days of the invoice date.
The supplier offers a 2/10 early-payment discount: pay within 10 days and take 2% off the invoice.
Taking the discount on the expected $3.0 million of annual purchases saves about $60,000 a year, but ties up cash 50 days earlier.

Commitments
There is no minimum purchase commitment. Each purchase order (PO) is binding once the supplier confirms it.
Price changes after the first twelve months are capped at 4% per year.
